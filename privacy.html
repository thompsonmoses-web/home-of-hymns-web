Privacy Policy

Home of Hymns · Last updated 3 November 2026

Home of Hymns is a free hymn-book application. We believe in being straightforward about data: we do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.

1. Data stored on your device only

Your profile details (name, phone or email, church/branch, photo), favourite hymns, reading history, and display preferences are saved in your own browser's local storage (localStorage). This data never leaves your device — clearing your browser data removes it permanently. We cannot see it, recover it, or share it.

2. No accounts, no servers, no tracking

The site has no user accounts, no databases, and no analytics or tracking scripts of our own. Hymn texts, tunes, and translations are bundled with the app and read directly by your browser.

3. Advertising

To keep the app free, we may display advertisements served by third-party advertising networks (such as Adsterra). These networks may use cookies or similar technologies to personalise the ads you see. Ad networks process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Adsterra Privacy Policy

You can opt out of personalised advertising with tools such as the NAI opt-out or your browser's cookie controls.

4. Third-party links

The app may link to external resources (for example, hymn-reference websites). We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites.

5. Children's privacy

Because no personal data is collected at all, the app is suitable for users of all ages, including children, under supervision.

6. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated accordingly.

7. Contact

Questions? Open an issue on our GitHub repository: thompsonmoses-web/home-of-hymns.
